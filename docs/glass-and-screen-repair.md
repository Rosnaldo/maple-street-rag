# Glass cutting and screen repair

## Window screens
- Rescreening a frame up to 36 x 48 inches: **$18**.
- Larger frames, up to 48 x 72 inches: **$25**.
- Ready in **3 business days**. Drop off at the service counter.

## Glass cutting
- Single-strength clear glass, cut to size: **$6 per square foot**, with a **$10 minimum**.
- We cut while you wait, about 10 minutes.
- We do **not** cut tempered, insulated (double-pane) or mirror glass.
