# Maple Rewards

Joining is free: give your phone number at the register.

- You earn **1 point for every whole dollar** you spend, before tax.
- **200 points = $10 off** a future purchase. Rewards are redeemed in blocks of 200 points only.
- Points expire **12 months** after you earn them.
- Rentals, gift cards and propane exchanges do not earn points.
