# Delivery

We deliver on **Tuesdays and Fridays** only. Order by 2:00 PM the day before.

| Distance from the store | Order subtotal (before tax) | Delivery fee |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 5 miles | Over $150 | Free |
| Up to 5 miles | $150 or less | $15 |
| More than 5, up to 15 miles | Any | $15 |
| More than 15 miles | Any | We do not deliver; pickup only |

Propane cannot be delivered.
