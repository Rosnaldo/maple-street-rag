# Special orders

If we do not stock it, we can usually order it from our supplier catalogs: larger tools, lawn equipment, appliances, special paint colors in bulk, and building materials.

- We take a **50% deposit** when you order.
- Most orders arrive in **7 to 10 business days**. We call you when it is here.
- We hold an arrived order for **14 days**.
- **Special orders cannot be returned** unless the item is defective.
- Special orders can be delivered under the normal delivery rules.
