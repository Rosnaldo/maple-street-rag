# Lumber and cutting service

Lumber, plywood and drywall are kept in the yard behind the store. Pull up to the yard gate and a team member will help you load.

## Cutting service
- We cut lumber and sheet goods on our panel saw and miter saw.
- The **first 2 cuts on each board or sheet are free**. Each cut after that is **$0.75**.
- Cuts are accurate to about 1/8 inch. For finish work, cut it yourself.
- We do not cut pressure-treated lumber shorter than 2 feet, or anything already cut by a customer.
- **Cut lumber cannot be returned.**

## Good to know
- A 4x8 sheet will not fit in most cars. Ask us to cut it down, rent a truck nearby, or use our delivery (Tuesdays and Fridays).
- Lumber is priced per board or per sheet, as in the price list.
- Pressure-treated lumber is for outdoor use. Wear gloves and a mask when cutting it.
