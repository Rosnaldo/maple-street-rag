# Store hours

Maple Street Hardware, 412 Maple Street.

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM |
| Saturday | 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM |
| Sunday | 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM |

## Holidays

- Closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day and New Year's Day.
- December 24 and December 31: we close early, at 3:00 PM.
- July 4: open 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

The key-cutting counter and the paint-mixing counter both stop taking new jobs 30 minutes before the store closes.
