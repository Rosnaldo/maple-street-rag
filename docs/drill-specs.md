# Cordless drill comparison

We carry two cordless drills.

## Cordless Drill 18V (tool only), $89.99
- 1/2 inch keyless chuck, 2 speeds, 350 in-lbs of torque.
- **Sold as the tool only: no battery and no charger in the box.**
- Uses our 18V Battery 2.0Ah ($49.99) and the 18V Battery Charger ($29.99), sold separately.
- A good choice if you already own batteries from the same 18V line.

## Cordless Drill 20V Brushless Kit, $149.00
- 1/2 inch metal chuck, 2 speeds, 530 in-lbs of torque, brushless motor for longer run time.
- Kit includes **two 4.0Ah batteries, a charger and a carrying bag.**
- The 20V batteries do not fit the 18V tools, and the 18V batteries do not fit the 20V tools.

## Warranty
Both drills carry a **3-year manufacturer warranty**. Within the first year, the store will exchange a defective drill on the spot with a receipt (see the return policy). After the first year, warranty claims go to the manufacturer; the service counter can help you file one.
