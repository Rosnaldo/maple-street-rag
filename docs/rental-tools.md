## How rental works
- A rental day runs until closing time the next day the store is open.
- A weekly rental (7 days) costs **4 times the daily rate**.
- You must be 18 or older and show photo ID.
- The deposit is taken on a credit or debit card. We do not take cash deposits.
- The deposit is refunded when the tool comes back clean and undamaged.
- Late returns are charged the daily rate for each extra day.
- A **$20 cleaning fee** is taken from the deposit if a tool comes back dirty.
- Rentals do not earn loyalty points.
