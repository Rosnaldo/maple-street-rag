# General questions

**Where do I park?** Free parking in the lot behind the store, entrance on Birch Lane. Two spaces by the side door are reserved for loading lumber; 15 minutes maximum.

**How can I pay?** Cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept personal checks. Contractor accounts can be billed (see the contractor account terms).

**Do you sell gift cards?** Yes, in $25, $50 and $100 amounts, at any register. Gift cards never expire and cannot be redeemed for cash.

**Can I bring my dog?** Yes. Dogs on a leash are welcome. Please keep them out of the paint-mixing area.

**Is there a restroom?** Yes, at the back of the store next to the service counter.

**Do you price match?** No. We keep our prices fair every day and run a flyer about every two weeks.

**Do you have a phone number?** Yes, (555) 010-4120. Staff answer during store hours.

**Do you sell lawn mowers or large appliances?** We do not stock them. Many can be special ordered; ask at the service counter.

**Where is the service counter?** At the back right of the store. Key cutting, sharpening, screen and glass repair, special orders and tool rental pickups all happen there.
