# Return policy

This policy takes effect March 1, 2026 and replaces all earlier return policies.

## With a receipt
Most items can be returned within **60 days** of purchase for a full refund to the original payment method.

## Without a receipt
Within 14 days of purchase we can give **store credit** for the lowest price the item sold for in the last 30 days. Photo ID is required.

## Power tools
- Unopened: same as any other item (60 days with a receipt).
- Opened: returnable within **30 days** with a receipt, with a **15% restocking fee**. The fee is waived if the tool is defective.

## Items we cannot take back
- Tinted or custom-mixed paint
- Anything cut to length for you: chain, rope, wire, lumber cut to size
- Propane cylinders and propane exchanges
- Gift cards

## Defective items
A defective item can be exchanged for the same item within one year of purchase with a receipt.
