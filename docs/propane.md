# Propane exchange

- We exchange **20 lb tanks only**, for $22.99. Bring your empty tank to the cage outside the front door and pay at any register.
- We do **not refill** tanks, and we do not sell new empty tanks.
- Tanks with a damaged valve, heavy rust, or a manufacture date more than **12 years** ago cannot be exchanged.
- You must be 18 or older.
- Propane is not returnable, does not earn rewards points, and cannot be delivered.
